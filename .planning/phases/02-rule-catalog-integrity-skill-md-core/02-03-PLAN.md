  sources.
- `### PF-2.3 — Adjacent evidence licenses an unhedged claim`: a claim whose evidence sits in the
  same or the next sentence is written flat and confident; a claim with no adjacent evidence is not
  repaired by hedging. State plainly, as the rule's own sentence, that hedging is never the repair
  for missing evidence — the repair is evidence or a marker. The `**Replace with:**` half names both
  directions: strip the hedge from an evidenced claim, and send an unevidenced one to `PF-2.4`.

  `GAP` marker goes in at the same position, so the omission is visible in place and not only in the
  register. The `**Replace with:**` half gives the marker's required contents: what is missing and
  why it is missing. State that the marker's rule token is the rule that raised it, which is what
  lets a check report cite an integrity finding exactly as it cites a prose violation.

  a volume, a performance level, or an outcome can become a contractual warranty, and the skill
  cannot judge whether it has. It raises `REVIEW (commitment)`. The `**Replace with:**` half names
