<objective>
Requirement CAT-10 says the frontmatter `description` acts as an explicit trigger list. Measured
live on 2026-09-20 it does not: 9 of 9 must-fire phrasings fired (correct) and 2 of 5 must-not-fire
phrasings also fired — a kickoff slide deck and a pricing/sizing calculation, both named in
`SKILL.md`'s own `## Limits` section and in `PROJECT.md`'s Out of Scope list, neither of which the
harness reads at activation time.

**This plan reopens a locked decision, and says so rather than slipping it through as a bug fix.**
Decision D-30 (`02-CONTEXT.md:84`) fixes the `description` as a front-loaded trigger list of roughly
400-600 characters; D-29 forbids framework marks in it. Both are flagged costly/one-way —
`03-RESEARCH.md:70` states the description's shape should not be reopened without a fresh
discuss-phase pass, because a published description propagates into registry indexes and forks that
do not re-fetch. This plan edits that field. The justification is that the field is now measurably
wrong on its own stated purpose, and the edit is additive: D-30's band and D-29's no-framework-marks
rule are both honoured, and the inclusion half — the part that measured 9 of 9 — is untouched.

**The fix is an experiment, not a repair, and the plan is written that way.** Hypothesis H4 in
`.planning/debug/DEBUG-cat10-trigger-over-fire.md` is untested: nothing in this repository shows
that an activation ranker applies a negative clause as a suppressor rather than simply adding the
excluded nouns to the surface it matches against. Under the semantic-matching hypothesis H1 the
clause could plausibly make the over-fires *worse*. Lexical pull is already refuted — the two
phrasings that fired share zero content words with the description — so the positive pull is
unidentified and adding a negation may do nothing at all.

The repository has already faced exactly this situation once and handled it well. The MOD-04
residual (`WINDOWS.md` id 8, full record in `evals/conformance/RESULTS-mod04.md` lines 60-120) put a
lever against a paired same-instrument baseline under a decision rule with numbered branches fixed
BEFORE the run; when the delta came in below threshold the honest finding — a decline — was recorded
and the residual waived and disclosed. This plan mirrors that structure exactly.

Purpose: move CAT-10 from "measured defect, mechanism unidentified" to a disposition selected by a
rule written before the number was known. Closing CAT-10 is one of six possible outcomes, not the
goal; recording a measured null is a real result and is pre-named as such.

Output: a pre-committed decision rule; an instrument that can run repeats without destroying
committed measurement history; 140 live paired sessions; a description whose trigger surface states
its own exclusions; and CAT-10, `WINDOWS.md` id 24 and gap G-02-2 all re-verdicted against what was
measured.

## The session budget, and the claim it supports

**Chosen: n=5 per phrasing, PAIRED, 140 planned live sessions (70 per arm).** This is the
statistically honest figure `DEBUG-cat10-trigger-over-fire.md` recommends, and no weaker claim is
being smuggled in behind a smaller number.

What n=5 buys, computed during planning with a stdlib `math.comb` script (these exact values are
Task 2's self-test fixtures):

| zero fires in n sessions | Clopper-Pearson upper bound on the true fire rate, alpha 0.05 |
|---|---|
| n=1 | 0.9500 |
| n=3 | 0.6316 |
| **n=5** | **0.4507** |
| n=10 | 0.2589 |
| n=20 | 0.1391 |
| n=59 | 0.0495 |

A clean must-not-fire row at n=5 therefore bounds that phrasing's true fire rate at 0.45, not at
zero. That is the strongest per-row statement this round can make and it is the statement that must
appear in the results — never "the description no longer over-fires".

Why not n=3 (84 paired sessions, the `evals/benchmark/` in-repo precedent)? A bound of 0.63 is
barely narrower than the n=1 bound of 0.95 that produced the current unsatisfying state. Why not
n=10 (280 sessions)? The marginal bound gain (0.45 to 0.26) does not justify doubling a quota
exposure that interrupted the `03-08` run twice.

Why both arms and not just the new description: without a pre-fix rate on the same instrument there
is no denominator. Fisher exact two-tailed on the pooled must-not-fire 2x2, computed during planning:

| control fires / sessions | treatment | p |
|---|---|---|
| 2 of 25 | 0 of 25 | 0.4898 |
| 3 of 25 | 0 of 25 | 0.2347 |
| 4 of 25 | 0 of 25 | 0.1099 |
| 5 of 25 | 0 of 25 | 0.0502 |
| 10 of 25 | 0 of 25 | 0.0006 |

So even a perfect treatment sweep is not attributable unless the control arm reproduces the defect
at 6 or more of its 25 must-not-fire sessions. That is a real limit of this round and the decision
rule below states it in advance rather than discovering it afterwards.

## What the existing instrument can and cannot do

Read `evals/trigger/run_trigger_test.py` in full before Task 2. The load-bearing facts:

- `SCOPE_HASH_LINES = 14`, and `recorded_scope_hash()` takes the **first** 64-hex token in
  `pressure-tests.md`. `main()` halts on mismatch **before opening a single session** (lines
  380-384). Re-authoring the hash is a precondition for the treatment arm, not a cleanup after it.
- `parse_tables()` reads the 14 phrasings out of `pressure-tests.md` itself. A cell that opens with
  a quote character breaks row detection — `02-05` recorded this.
- `detect_activation()` reads a `Skill` tool-use event naming `proof-first` out of the session's own
  stream. It does not read prose. Do not change this.
- `run_session()` copies **only** `skills/proof-first/` into the temp session's
  `.claude/skills/`. Nothing under `.claude-plugin/`, `output-styles/` or `prompts/` is present in
  any measured session — which is why those files cannot confound the measurement.
- `write_results()` **clobbers** `--out` with `write_text()`. There is no append mode and no
  overwrite guard. Running the existing script against the default `--out` would destroy the
  2026-09-20 measurement. This is the single largest accident risk in the plan and Task 2 closes it.
- There is no `--repeats`. One session per row is all it can do today.
- `--transcripts <dir>` already exists (line 361) and defaults to a `tempfile.mkdtemp()` that is
  never kept. The 2026-09-20 run's `system/init` events are gone, which is why Q3 and H3 in the
  debug session could not be settled by measurement.
- `--jobs` defaults to 3, `--timeout` to 600, `--model` to `claude-sonnet-5`. **Both arms must use
  the same values** — same instrument means same instrument.

**Counters.** For arm X and pressure-test row r: `S_X(r)` is r's scoreable session count in arm X
(unscoreable sessions excluded from numerator and denominator alike) and `F_X(r)` is the count of
those in which the skill fired. `MNF` is the 5 must-not-fire rows, `MF` the 9 must-fire rows.

- `OF_X` = sum of `F_X(r)` over MNF — over-fires, 0..25 at n=5
- `SN_X` = sum of `S_X(r)` over MNF
- `MH_X` = sum of `F_X(r)` over MF — must-fire hits, 0..45
- `SM_X` = sum of `S_X(r)` over MF
- Arm **B** = control = the unchanged 439-character description. Arm **A** = treatment = the
  551-character description above.
- `p_attr` = two-tailed Fisher exact on `[[OF_B, SN_B - OF_B], [OF_A, SN_A - OF_A]]`

**Sample floors** (80 percent of planned): `SN_A >= 20`, `SN_B >= 20`, `SM_A >= 36`, `SM_B >= 36`.
**Attempt cap:** 170 live invocations total across both arms (140 planned plus up to 30 retries).
**Closure threshold:** `OF_A == 0` and `F_A(r) == S_A(r)` for every r in MF.
**Attribution threshold:** `p_attr < 0.05`. Below it, the round may still close CAT-10 on Arm A's own
measurement, but the annotation must state in as many words that the improvement is not
distinguishable from the control at this sample size.

## Trust Boundaries

This plan drives a live model harness and writes the result into the repository's own evidence
files. The boundary that matters is between **what was measured** and **what the repository claims**.
Three crossings: model output entering the results files (Tasks 3 and 5), a plan-authored figure
entering shipped content (Task 4), and a measured number selecting a published verdict (Task 6). No
network service, credential or user input is involved; `claude -p` runs under the operator's existing
login and every session runs in a temp directory outside the repository.

## STRIDE Threat Register

| # | Threat | Category | Mitigation in this plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | A verdict is recorded that no session produced | Spoofing | Verdicts are read from each session's own event stream, never from its prose; transcripts are retained and distilled into `INIT-EVENTS.md` so any cell can be traced back to a session |
