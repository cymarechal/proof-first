<objective>
Close G-04-7 — the three warnings `04-REVIEW.md` raised and the phase deferred — so the two tools that
enforce this repository's distribution guarantees stop carrying defects of the exact kind they exist to
catch in everything else.

Purpose: all three are latent rather than live, which is why the phase shipped past them, and all three
are the same shape of problem. `_owner_segment` promises to compare a GitHub account segment and
instead compares a string prefix, so two carriers naming the same repository in different URL syntaxes
would be reported as disagreeing. `generate_derivatives.py --check` promises a byte-for-byte comparison
in its own docstring and performs a text comparison with universal-newline translation, so the guard
described as the second independent check on derivative freshness cannot see a line-ending difference,
and the writer does not pin its output newline. Two plugin-manifest checks silently do less work when a
repository ships anything other than exactly one skill folder, with no stated ceiling and no violation
raised. A repository whose argument is that a claim is worth what its evidence is worth cannot ship a
checker that overstates what it checks.

Output: a normalising owner extractor proven in both directions, a genuinely byte-comparing `--check`
with a platform-pinned writer, two disclosed ceilings and one new firing condition inside an existing
code. No new violation code; the discrimination-proven total stays at 47.
</objective>
