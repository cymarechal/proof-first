# 06-07 — Gap closure for Phase 6 UAT, round 3

## Why this plan exists

Round 3 ran the independent read that LEG-04 and LEG-05 both name as their closure condition. Five
readers, none of whom had written the text they read. The fixes from round 2 came back clean — all
fourteen of them, re-checked against the files the original readers had cited. The files did not:
eleven checkably-false statements, opening G-06-10, G-06-11 and G-06-12.

The distribution matters more than the count, because it says what to do differently rather than
just what to fix:

| Where the eleven came from | Count |
|---|---|
| 06-05 text that round 2's briefs pointed no reader at (`0b8a865`, `7260702`) | 4 |
| Files no brief had ever named, found only by the new sweep reader (`da0bd98`, `b627fd3`, `c4efe2e`) | 3 |
| Authored by the closing round itself while fixing something else (`b931161`, `a18f492`, `d56022c`, `3a37839`) | 4 |
| Regressions of a round-2 fix | **0** |

Attribution is `git blame` on the line each finding cites, not recollection. One of the four
closing-round statements — `README.md`:9-11 — imported a claim that has sat in
`evals/benchmark/bench-deal-brief.md`:8 unread since Phase 5 (`1577910`).

Zero regressions. The corrections are holding; the reading was too narrow. Three rounds have now each
found a class the round before could not see, and the new whole-tree sweep brief — bound to no file —
returned three findings in three files at the same cost as a file-scoped reader. That brief is now
permanent, and it is task 12 of this plan rather than an afterthought.

One finding deserves to be stated plainly before the tasks, because it is not a wording defect and
should not be fixed like one. `LEGAL-REVIEW.md` classifies six of the eight MC dimension names as
ordinary business English on its way to a prong-4 disposition. `tools/check_repo.py` classifies seven
of the eight as source-coined and fails the build if any of them reaches shipped skill content. Both
are this repository's own considered positions on the same eight strings, and they are opposites.
Task 1 is not a rewrite; it is a decision about which one this repository holds.

## Tasks

### Task 1 — Reconcile the prong-4 classification with what the checker enforces (G-06-10 finding 1)

The conflict, stated exactly:

- `LEGAL-REVIEW.md`:249-252 — "Metric", "Pain", "Champion", "Competition", "Decision Criteria" and
  "Decision Process" are "ordinary business English that stands on its own outside this framework
  family"; only "Economic Buyer" and "Paper Process" are terms of art.
- `LEGAL-REVIEW.md`:308 — "They are not neutral English the way "Metric" or "Competition" are."
- `tools/check_repo.py`:2952-2965 — `SOURCE_COINED_LABELS` holds `economic buyer`, `paper process`,
  `decision criteria`, `decision process`, `champion`, `competition`, `pain`. Its own comment says
  `metric` is "deliberately excluded" as "the ordinary-English word for a measurement".
  `check_source_label_in_skill_content` (`:2977-3009`) makes each of the seven a build failure in
  shipped skill content, on the stated ground that it is a source-coined label.

Decide which position this repository holds, then make the other file agree. Two permitted outcomes:

1. **The checker is right.** Restate `:249-252` on the seven-of-eight split, and re-run the prong-4
   reasoning on it — which means conceding the prong for seven names rather than two, and saying what
   follows. `:308`'s "Competition" example must go. Expect the conclusion to move; if it does not,
   say why in the entry rather than leaving the reader to notice.
2. **The two answer different questions.** `check_repo.py`'s list governs running prose in shipped
   skill content; `NUMBERING.md`'s registry labels are explicitly out of its scope (`:302-305` routes
   exactly that question to this review). If that distinction is what makes both classifications
   right, state it in `LEGAL-REVIEW.md` at the point of the split, name the checker and its scope
   line, and explain why a term can be source-coined in prose and ordinary English as a block label.

Do not split the difference silently. Whichever outcome, the entry must name `SOURCE_COINED_LABELS`
and its seven members, so the next reader meets the conflict already answered.

**Verify:** `LEGAL-REVIEW.md`'s split and `check_repo.py`'s tuple can be read together without
contradiction; the entry cites the constant by name; `python3 tools/check_repo.py` is 0 violations.
