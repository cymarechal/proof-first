The measurement behind that decision, its rejected alternative and its reopening condition are in

## Why this round is shaped differently

Rounds 1-6 closed findings in shipped files. This round's centre of mass moved: **twelve of
twenty-three findings are in `.planning/`**, and three of those are ledger rows whose
`LEGAL-REVIEW.md` twins 06-10 already corrected. The defect is not that the ledger drifted — it is
that **the correction was applied to the reproduction and not to the original**. `WINDOWS.md` is the
ledger; `LEGAL-REVIEW.md` reproduces it. Any task here that corrects a ledger claim corrects
`WINDOWS.md` first, through its JSON fence, and re-renders.

Two rules carry forward from 06-10 and both bind this round:

- **Change 5** — re-run every command literal this round commits into prose *after* the SUMMARY
  lands. It caught this round's UAT write-up: all five of its `06-UAT.md` self-citations broke
  between being written and being committed.
- **Plan figures are claims, not inputs.** Every `**Verify:**` below is run *before* its edit. Where
  this plan states a number, the task's first act is to re-derive it; a disagreement is recorded as a
  deviation, not silently reconciled.
